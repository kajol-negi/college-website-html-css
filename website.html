The H.S.B.M. JAIN GIRLS INTER COLLEGE for Girls believes that all learning must be purposeful. It should lead to the development of responsible citizens possessing qualities of honesty, sincerity and respect for others. The college encourages students to grow academically and morally for the betterment of society.
